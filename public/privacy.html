void
privacy policy · effective may 2025

the short version

void does not store your messages. ever. the server cannot read them — they are end-to-end encrypted before leaving your browser. when a room closes, nothing remains.

what we collect

  • IP address — used transiently for rate limiting only (max 10 rooms/hour, 30 messages/minute per user). it is never logged, stored on disk, or shared with anyone.
  • Room join name — sent to the server once when you join a room so your peer can see who connected. not stored after the room ends.
nothing above is written to a database or file. if the server restarts, all of it vanishes. this is by design.

what we do not collect

  • message content (encrypted client-side before transmission)
  • account information (there are no accounts)
  • cookies or tracking tokens
  • device fingerprints or browser information
  • analytics or usage telemetry
  • third-party scripts of any kind

how encryption works

every message is encrypted in your browser before it reaches our server using:

  • ECDH P-256 — key exchange between you and your peer
  • HKDF + SHA-256 — derives a unique AES key for your session
  • AES-GCM-256 — authenticated encryption of every message

the server acts as a relay only. it sees encrypted bytes it cannot decrypt. your private keys never leave your browser.

verify this yourself: open devtools → network tab → inspect websocket frames. you will see only base64-encoded ciphertext.

your name in localstorage

if you enter a name, it is saved in your browser's localStorage so you don't have to retype it. it stays on your device only. it is cleared when you leave a room. you can clear it anytime by clearing site data in your browser settings.

data retention

rooms are deleted automatically after 24 hours or when all users leave — whichever comes first. there is no database. no logs. no backups. once a room is gone, nothing can recover it.

third parties

void uses no third-party analytics, advertising networks, CDNs, or embedded scripts. all assets are served from our own server. we do not sell, share, or monetise any data.
